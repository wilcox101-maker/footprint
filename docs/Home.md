# Footprint

Footprint is a local web app for looking up what is already public about a domain, URL, email address, username, phone number, or IP address. Findings stay in the browser that ran the scan. Nothing is uploaded to a Footprint server, because there is not one.

Use it on your own assets, or on a target you are allowed to research. The app states that on every investigation. Unauthorized scanning is your responsibility, not the software’s.

## What a scan gives you

* An overall risk of Critical, High, Medium, Low, or Info, plus a count of each.
* Findings with a plain explanation and a fix, when a fix exists.
* Breach **titles** and data **categories** for an email. Not passwords, not dump rows, not a Social Security number.
* Public profile hits, certificate names, DNS, routing, archives, and links you open yourself.
* A Footprint tab with official opt-out and takedown pages, and a letter you can copy if a form is ignored.

## What it is not

Footprint is not a port scanner, an exploit runner, or a dark-web crawler. It does not connect to Tor. It does not locate a handset. It does not pull stolen-credential dumps. A commercial product that shows one row per leaked password will show a higher Critical count, because Footprint counts breach titles the public indexes will return, and it never retrieves the password.
